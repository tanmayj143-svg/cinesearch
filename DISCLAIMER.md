## Recommended before production use

1. Read the code, especially anything touching credentials, network calls, or
   filesystem/database writes (`worker/ingest.py`, `api/main.py`, `schema/`).
2. Add an authentication layer before any non-trusted network exposure.
3. Keep `$LIBRARY_ROOT` and `$HF_CACHE` on the intended drive (the preflight
   script checks this); a full root filesystem will silently break ingest.
4. Verify the model licences you rely on (ShotVL, Qwen, CLAP, BGE-M3, Whisper)
   suit your use — several carry research or non-commercial terms.
5. Back up the Postgres volume before upgrades.
